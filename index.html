Privacy Policy

StockPilot

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed StockPilot as an inventory and point of sale tool for small businesses. It includes a product catalog with variants, barcodes, lots, and expiration dates, a barcode scanner, stock counts and adjustments, a point of sale (POS) with receipts, purchases and suppliers, customers and customer credit, expenses, stock alerts, smart restocking, analytics and reports, labels, product import from Excel or CSV, report export, and backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. The barcode scanner uses Google ML Kit, which analyzes camera images on the device. These services never receive the business information or the customer and supplier data recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Business profile: business name, business type, and logo
  • Products: name, category, variants, SKU and barcode, cost and sale prices, stock, minimum stock, lots and expiration dates, notes, and product photos
  • Inventory movements, stock adjustments, and stock counts
  • Sales, returns, receipts, discounts, payment methods, and payments received
  • Purchases and purchase orders, and payments made to suppliers
  • Customers and suppliers: name, contact person, phone number, email address, tax ID, address, notes, credit limit, and balances
  • Expenses, accounts receivable, and accounts payable
  • Currencies and exchange rates entered by the user
  • Preferences: language, theme, currency, number and date formats, alert settings, receipt footer, backup settings, and app lock settings

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share a report, a receipt, a purchase order, labels, or a message, or to make a backup using the features available in the application.

To suggest the currency and the number and date formats, the application reads the country code of the SIM card and the mobile network, and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS), happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

The sample data offered when setting up the application is created on the device and can be used to explore the features.

Information About the User's Customers and Suppliers

When the user records their customers and suppliers, they enter personal information about other people. This information is stored only on the user's device and Compose R.A Studio never receives it.

The user decides what information to record about their customers and suppliers and is responsible for handling it in accordance with the laws that apply to them, for example by informing their customers and recording only the information needed to run their business. The application helps by keeping this information on the device, by allowing it to be edited or removed at any time, and by offering an optional app lock.

What the Application Does Not Do

StockPilot:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), contacts, microphone, or SMS messages
  • Does not access the photo gallery: when the user adds a product photo or a logo, Android's photo picker gives the application access only to the image the user selects
  • Does not use the camera unless the user scans a barcode or takes a product photo
  • Does not send, sell, or share the business information or the customer and supplier data recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, during the first 24 hours after the application is installed, or at the point of sale while the user is serving a customer. They appear only occasionally, when the user completes an action such as saving a product or exporting a report, after several actions and with a minimum time between two advertisements. When the action opens another application, such as sharing a report, the advertisement is shown first.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The business information and the customer and supplier data recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google ML Kit (Barcode Scanning)

The barcode scanner uses Google ML Kit to read barcodes from the camera image. The images are analyzed on the device and are not sent to Google or to Compose R.A Studio. ML Kit may contact Google servers to receive updates and may send Google metrics about the performance and use of the scanning feature, which Google uses to measure performance, maintain and improve the service, and detect misuse. More information: ML Kit Terms and data disclosure.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Reports (PDF, Excel, and CSV): sales, inventory, product, and business summary reports can be shared through the Android share menu or saved in the location the user chooses.
  • Receipts, purchase orders, and labels (PDF): they can be shared, saved, or printed. Printing uses Android's print service and the printer the user selects.
  • Contacting a customer or supplier: the call, WhatsApp, and email buttons open the corresponding application with the phone number or email address and, where applicable, a prepared message. Nothing is sent until the user confirms it in that application.
  • Importing products: the Excel or CSV file the user selects is read on the device to add products. The application only accesses the file the user chooses.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share or print a file, the application creates a temporary copy in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From More → Data → Backups, the user can create a backup file (.spbackup) with all the information of the application: products, photos, inventory, sales, purchases, customers, suppliers, finances, logo, and preferences. The file is created only when the user requests it, and it is shared or saved in the location the user chooses. If the user turns on automatic backups, the application saves backups periodically in the folder the user selects and keeps the number of copies the user chooses. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The backup file is not encrypted and contains the personal information of the user's customers and suppliers, so it should be kept in a safe place. It does not include the PIN or the app lock settings. Restoring a backup replaces the information currently stored in the application. Before restoring, the application keeps a safety copy of the current information in its private storage.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the application's database, settings, product photos, and logo in the backup of the user's Google account, or copy them when moving to a new device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Stock Alerts and Notifications

When inventory alerts are enabled, the application checks on the device, once a day and when stock changes, which products are out of stock, low on stock, expired, or about to expire, and shows a notification. These notifications may include the number of affected products and the names of some of them, and may appear on the lock screen depending on the device's notification settings.

Alerts can be turned off at any time in More → Settings → Privacy and alerts → Notifications, and notifications can also be disabled from the device settings.

Home Screen Widget

The optional home screen widget shows today's sales, the inventory value, and the alerts, so this information is visible to anyone who can see the device's home screen. When the app lock is on, the widget hides the amounts. The widget is added and removed by the user from the device's home screen.

App Lock and Biometric Authentication

StockPilot offers an optional app lock, in More → Settings → Privacy and alerts → Security & lock, that protects access to the application with a PIN and, optionally, with the device's fingerprint or face unlock, depending on the device's capabilities.

The PIN is never stored as entered: the application keeps only a one-way cryptographic hash of it (PBKDF2) in its private storage, which is not included in backup files. Biometric authentication is processed entirely by the Android operating system, using the device's secure authentication framework (BiometricPrompt). The application only receives whether the authentication succeeded. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, transmit, or access any biometric data, including fingerprints or facial data.

The Hide in recent apps option prevents the application's content from being visible in the recent apps screen.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. To protect it further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device, to enable the app lock, and to keep backup files in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit, archive, or delete products, customers, suppliers, expenses, and other records from within the application, as available on each screen
  • Turn off automatic backups and delete backup files from the folder where they were saved
  • Delete all the information by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, reports, receipts, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.
